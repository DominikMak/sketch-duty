
# Sketch Duty
## Sketch Duty is a 2D deduction game for PC in which the police sketch you make becomes the evidence you later have to rely on.
### Description:
You are a beat cop suddenly reassigned as the department's sketch artist, because the long-time artist quit to teach painting to monkeys full time. You interview witnesses and build a composite of the suspect. Weeks later you pick him out of a lineup and break him in interrogation, and your sketch is only as good as the witnesses and your judgment of them.
